The Context

The customer sees speed. Operations maps the grid.

Amazon Now promises fresh groceries and daily essentials in 30 minutes or less. Behind that experience is a massive shift in supply chain architecture, moving products closer to the customer than ever before.

The 30-Minute Challenge

Delivering perishables and essentials within a 30-minute window means traditional linehaul transportation models no longer work.

The operation must rely on a fragmented network of specialized, smaller locations integrated directly into populated urban and suburban areas. Pickers must grab items in minutes, and couriers must be dispatched instantly.

How do you orchestrate speed, safety, and inventory accuracy when your total operational buffer is less than 30 minutes?
Product Insight

Proximity is Product.

Fast delivery is not just about moving vehicles faster. It is about predictive inventory routing—ensuring the right avocados, electronics, and essentials are already sitting in a 2-mile radius of the customer before they even order.
