01 / CASE STUDY

PRODUCT DESIGN · B2B COMMERCE

Lyncc: Streamlining
B2B Organic
Ingredient Sourcing

Role: Product Analysis · UI/UX · App Testing

01

Project Overview

Lyncc is a dedicated B2B mobile application designed to simplify the complex supply chain of bulk organic ingredients. It serves as a bridge between suppliers and buyers looking to source high-quality grains, spices, herbs, and seeds.

The platform digitizes the entire procurement workflow—from discovering ingredients and reviewing strict quality specifications (like moisture and purity levels) to uploading purchase orders and tracking shipments in real-time.

My involvement centered on application testing, business data analysis, and refining the user experience to ensure a seamless, error-free journey for enterprise buyers placing high-value orders.

02

The Challenge

Bulk sourcing is chaotic.
The procurement app shouldn't be.

B2B buyers face immense friction when sourcing organic commodities. They typically rely on scattered spreadsheets, manual email chains for Purchase Orders (POs), and have zero visibility into their shipment's transit status.

01

Complex Specifications

Buyers need immediate access to critical product details like origin, grade, foreign matter percentage, and curcumin levels before ordering.

02

Manual PO Processing

Transitioning from an analog "Purchase Order" upload system to a streamlined digital checkout requires careful handling of document uploads.

03

Opaque Tracking

High-volume orders (e.g., 20 Metric Tons) demand transparent, stage-by-stage logistical tracking from dispatch to delivery.

03

Designing the Experience

The interface was built with a minimal, clutter-free UI to prioritize readability and trust. A distinct yellow accent guides the user through the primary conversion funnel.

01. Secure Onboarding

A simple, secure entry point. The login and signup flows require essential enterprise user information while keeping the interface clean and maintaining consistent brand typography.

03

The Platform Flow

A seamless 6-step journey from intake to compliance.

Step 1

Quantity & Lots

Tracking the contracted volume versus delivered year-to-date. The UI utilizes clear, high-contrast metric cards and a minimalist bar chart to give supply-chain leads instant visibility into their quarterly projections.

Step 2

Quality Grades

Locking in the spec sheet at intake. This view maps key parameters like Curcumin content and Moisture against EU specifications, using simple 'Pass/Fail' status indicators to reduce cognitive load.

Step 3

Documents & Certificates

An automated document repository. All required paperwork—from EU Organic transaction certificates to Commercial invoices—are automatically generated and attached to the specific lot.

Step 4

Lab & Residue Testing

Visualizing third-party lab results. I designed custom progress bars to show exactly where pesticide and heavy metal levels sit relative to strict EU detection limits.

Step 5

Logistics & Shipping

A dynamic timeline tracking the container's journey from the origin warehouse in Nizamabad to the final discharge in Rotterdam. Key shipping data like the Bill of Lading is kept front and center.

Step 6

Compliance Status

The final gatekeeper. A grid of automated checks ensures the lot is EUDR deforestation-free and HACCP compliant before it is flagged as 'Clear to ship'.

04

Outcome

Streamlined B2B Operations.

By integrating clear data visualization for product specs and a frictionless PO upload system, the app drastically reduces the time it takes to place and verify wholesale organic orders.

Data-Driven Refinement.

Rigorous app testing and functional workflow validation ensured that edge cases in the ordering process were identified and resolved prior to deployment.
